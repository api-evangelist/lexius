# Lexius

> Lexius is a Y Combinator-backed AI-powered loss prevention platform that turns existing retail security cameras into proactive, revenue-protecting systems. It detects shoplifting in real-time, sends instant mobile alerts with video clips, and provides traceback search across all camera feeds.

Lexius helps physical retail stores reduce shrinkage and protect revenue without replacing any hardware. The AI connects to existing security cameras and NVR/VMS systems from any brand, and is typically set up remotely in under 24 hours.

## Key Features
- Real-time shoplifting detection (concealment, product sweeping, known offender entry)
- Instant mobile alerts with video clips
- Traceback search: natural-language search across all cameras (e.g., "Man in blue jacket near electronics")
- Automatic case file building for law enforcement
- Chain-wide intelligence: known offenders flagged across all connected stores
- Slip-and-fall detection for liability protection
- Compatible with 11+ camera brands including Hikvision, Axis, Dahua, Hanwha, Bosch, Honeywell, Uniview, Lorex, LTS, Avigilon, and Genetec
